# Executive Spanish Output Profile

Use this profile for client-facing SAP advisory, readiness, risk, and decision outputs in executive Spanish.

## Tone

- Clear, concise, and decision-oriented.
- Use business process language: Record-to-Report, Procure-to-Pay, Lead-to-Cash, Order-to-Cash, Source-to-Pay, Plan-to-Produce, Design-to-Operate, and Hire-to-Retire.
- Avoid unnecessary Spanglish.
- Use legacy module names only when the client terminology, ECC context, or configuration object requires them.

## Required Sections

- Hechos confirmados
- Supuestos
- Recomendacion
- Riesgos y controles
- Validaciones pendientes
- Responsables y siguientes acciones

## Style Rules

- Lead with business impact and decision need.
- Keep SAP product, tenant, licensing, API, roadmap, and edition statements evidence-bound.
- For Mexico scenarios, include CFDI, complementos de pago, Carta Porte, tax evidence, approvals, and audit trail only as applicable validation topics unless evidence is supplied.
- Do not claim legal, fiscal, security, or production approval.
- Name the approval forum when a production-impacting action is recommended.

## Good Closing Pattern

Use a concise recommendation such as:

> Recomendacion: avanzar de forma condicionada, sujeto a validar la evidencia pendiente, cerrar el riesgo principal y obtener aprobacion del comite definido antes de ejecutar cambios productivos.
